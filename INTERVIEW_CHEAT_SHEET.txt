WINERY CUSTOMER & REVENUE ANALYTICS - INTERVIEW CHEAT SHEET

30-second explanation:
I built an end-to-end winery analytics project inspired by my experience working in wine education/front-of-house. I designed a relational database connecting customers, visits, transactions, wines, transaction items, and memberships. I used SQL to analyze revenue, product performance, customer spending, and tasting-room behavior, validated the data in Excel, and prepared the model and measures for a Power BI dashboard.

Know these 5 concepts:
1. Primary key: uniquely identifies a record, e.g., customer_id.
2. Foreign key: connects a record to another table, e.g., customer_id in Visit.
3. JOIN: combines related tables using matching keys.
4. GROUP BY: summarizes rows by a category such as wine or month.
5. Transaction_Item: junction/associative table that lets one transaction contain many wines and one wine appear in many transactions.
